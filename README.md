## 🚀 Installation & Deployment

Host your own Child Panel using Render.

### 1. Deploy Child Panel

**Step 1: Copy the GitHub Repository URL**

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Copy%20Repository-181717?logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/pradhanrudrapratab-sketch/mrkrishna-child-panel)

Repository URL:
```text
https://github.com/pradhanrudrapratab-sketch/mrkrishna-child-panel.git
```

**Step 2: Create a Render Account**

[![Render](https://img.shields.io/badge/Render-Create%20Account-46E3B7?logo=render&logoColor=white)](https://render.com)

**Step 3: Create a New Web Service**

1. Open your Render Dashboard.
2. Click **New → Web Service**.
3. Paste your GitHub repository URL.
4. Select the **Free ($0) plan**.
5. Click **Deploy Web Service**.
6. Wait for the deployment to complete.

Your Child Panel website is now published!

[![Render Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/Render-Open%20Dashboard-46E3B7?logo=render&logoColor=white)](https://dashboard.render.com/)

---

### 2. Authorize Parent Panel

After successful deployment:

1. Open your newly deployed Child Panel website.
2. Click **Authorize Parent Panel**.
3. You will be redirected to the MrKrishna Parent Panel.
4. Click **Authorize** to approve the connection.
5. You will automatically return to your Child Panel.

[![MrKrishna Parent Panel](https://img.shields.io/badge/MrKrishna-Open%20Parent%20Panel-6C3BFF?logoColor=white)](https://mrkrishna-smm-panel.onrender.com)

---

### 3. Create Your Admin Account

After authorization:

1. Enter your email address.
2. Create a new admin password.
3. Complete the setup.

Your Child Panel admin account is now ready!

## 💳 Payment Gateway Configuration

Configure your own FamPay payment details to receive customer payments directly.

### Step 1: Get Your UPI ID

1. Open your FamApp.
2. Copy your personal UPI ID.
3. Keep it ready for configuration.

### Step 2: Generate Gmail App Password

Your Gmail account needs an App Password for email-based payment notification verification.

1. Open your Google Account.
2. Search for **App Passwords**.
3. Create a new app.
4. Enter the app name: `mksmm`.
5. Click **Next**, then **Create**.
6. Copy the generated 16-character App Password.

[![Google App Passwords](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google-Create%20App%20Password-4285F4?logo=google&logoColor=white)](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)

[![Gmail](https://img.shields.io/badge/Gmail-Open%20Gmail-EA4335?logo=gmail&logoColor=white)](https://mail.google.com/)

**Note:** Google App Passwords require an eligible Google Account with 2-Step Verification enabled.

### Step 3: Configure Payment Gateway

Open your Child Panel:

**Settings → Payment Gateway**

Enter the following information:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| UPI ID | Your FamPay UPI ID |
| Gmail Email | Your FamPay-registered Gmail address |
| Gmail App Password | Generated App Password |
| FamPay Sender Email | `no-reply@famapp.in` |

Save your settings.

Your payment gateway configuration is complete!

## 🔐 Security Guidelines

- Never share your Gmail App Password with anyone.
- Never expose payment credentials in frontend code.
- Keep your admin password secure.
- Use HTTPS for your deployed website.
- Only trust verified FamPay payment notifications.
- Always verify the payment amount and transaction details before confirming a payment.
- Store sensitive credentials securely on the server.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Render's free tier has usage and sleep limitations.
- Your Child Panel depends on the availability of the MrKrishna Parent Panel.
- Gmail App Passwords may not be available for every Google Account.
- Test payment verification before accepting customer payments.
- Check database persistence and backup requirements before using free-tier hosting for production.
